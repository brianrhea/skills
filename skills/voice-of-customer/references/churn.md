# Cancellation reasons

**Good for:** a first list of reasons to investigate, and the moment a customer decided to go.

**Distorts:** the stated reason is shaped by the cancel screen. A dropdown offers a few socially easy answers, and "too expensive" often stands in for "not getting enough from it". People leaving rarely write much.

**How to read them**
- Keep the dropdown choice and the free text as separate fields. Count them separately.
- When someone says price, look at what else they wrote and record the reason behind it if they give one.
- Add tenure and plan to each record when the export has them. Leaving in the first month is a different story from leaving after two years.
- Report these as stated reasons. To learn the real ones, interview people who cancelled in the last 90 days and nominate them in the report.
