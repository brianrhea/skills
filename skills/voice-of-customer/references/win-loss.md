# Won and lost deals

**Good for:** patterns in which deals close, what was compared, and where deals stall.

**Distorts:** CRM reason codes and rep notes are the seller's view of the deal. Reps often do not know, or do not record, why a buyer chose otherwise. The buyer's own account is the real source and has to come from an interview.

**How to read them**
- Keep won, lost and no-decision deals apart. "No decision" usually means the status quo won, which is its own finding.
- Label each record with `"evidence": "buyer"` for words the buyer said on a recorded call or wrote in an email, and `"evidence": "seller"` for notes and reason codes. Report them separately, and never present a seller's note as a buyer's reason.
- Record each factor with a direction: it helped, it hurt, or it was raised and resolved.
- Do not mix time periods when the product or pricing changed between them.
- With fewer than about twenty deals in a segment, describe what you see without drawing a pattern.
- End with the deals most worth a buyer interview: recent, and with a reason that is unclear or contested.
