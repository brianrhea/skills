# Sales and success calls

**Good for:** how buyers describe their situation before they know the product's vocabulary, what they compare it with, and what they ask about before committing.

**Distorts:** the rep steers the conversation. Buyers in a sales call are polite and guarded, and what they say they will do is weaker evidence than what they have done.

**How to read them**
- Pass every rep, agent and interviewer name to `--interviewer` so their words cannot be quoted.
- Favour what the customer says about the past: what they tried, what went wrong, what they are using today.
- Treat "we would", "we plan to" and "that would be great" as weak. Mark those records `"strength": "stated"`.
- A discovery call often contains pieces of a switch story. Record `push`, `pull`, `anxiety` and `habit` when they appear, and leave the buying timeline to a proper interview.
- Note which other tools and workarounds are named. They feed competitive-analysis.
