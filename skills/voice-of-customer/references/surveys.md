# Survey comments and NPS

**Good for:** breadth. A large number of short statements in the customers' own words, usually with a score and some segment data attached.

**Distorts:** comments are short and lack context. The people who answer are not the people who ignore surveys, and the question asked shapes the answer.

**How to read them**
- Keep the score and segment fields with each record, so counts can be split by them.
- A short comment supports one observation at most.
- Read low and middling scores first. Praise tells you less about what to change.
- Report the response rate if the user knows it, and say who was asked.
