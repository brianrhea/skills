# Support tickets

**Good for:** what breaks, what confuses, what people try to do that the product does not support, and the words they use for it.

**Distorts:** tickets come from existing customers who cared enough to write. They over-represent problems with the current product and say almost nothing about why people buy or who never signed up. Volume follows the number of users of a feature as much as how bad it is.

**How to read them**
- Quote only what the customer wrote. Agent replies, macros and signatures are not evidence. If the export mixes them in one field, say so in the report.
- Record the underlying goal when the customer states it: "so I can send the report before Monday".
- A ticket where the customer describes their own fix is a `workaround`.
- Repeated identical text is usually one person or an outage. The prepare script sets exact duplicates aside; mention how many.
- Add `area` to each record for the part of the product involved, separate from `theme`.
