# Job interviews

Use this when nobody has bought anything yet, so there is no purchase to walk back through. You are talking to people who do the job today, with whatever they have. The aim is to learn how they get it done now and where it hurts.

The opening script, the screener rules and the interviewer techniques in the main skill all still apply. Only the body of the guide changes.

## Who to recruit

People who did the job recently, in the last few weeks. Screen for the situation, not the title. Five conversations is enough to see whether the problem is real.

## Five things to cover

Write one opening prompt and three or four follow-ups for each. Phrase them as "take me through" and "tell me about the last time", never "what are your main challenges".

| Objective | Open with | Listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How they do it today | "Take me through the last time you did this, from the start." | The real steps and their order, and the tools they reach for |
| Where it goes wrong | "Tell me about a time it went badly." | Obstacles, delays and the workarounds they built |
| How it feels | "What was going through your head at that point?" | What they want to feel or avoid feeling |
| Who else is involved | "Who else knew about this, or had a say?" | Who they want to look good to, and who can block a change |
| What they have already tried | "What have you tried to make this easier? What did you pay for?" | Evidence the problem is worth solving, and what failed |

## What to leave out

- "Would you use something that…?" People are generous about their future selves. Ask what they have already done.
- "If you could design the perfect…?" It produces a wish list, and wishes are not evidence.
- Any description of your idea until the last five minutes, and only if you choose to.

## Reading the answers

- "Nothing really happens if I don't sort it" means an inconvenience, not a problem.
- A workaround they built themselves means they care.
- Money or time already spent on it is the strongest signal there is.

If you have a `research/job-canvas.md`, bring its riskiest assumptions as the things to listen for, and do not read them out.
